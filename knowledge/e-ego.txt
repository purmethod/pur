E — EGO
PURE Method · Ego · the points Paul has defined (points 3 and 4 are not yet defined by Paul)

═══ E1 — KILLING THE EGO, WITHOUT DRUGS ═══
— Core principle —
The ego is not your enemy because it exists. It is your enemy because it runs the show without you noticing. Killing the ego does not mean destroying yourself. It means taking the wheel back from the part of you that needs to be right, admired and better than others.
Many men look for the shortcut: a psychedelic weekend, a ceremony, a substance that dissolves the self for one night. PURE does not go there. What a substance shows you for six hours, you have to build without it anyway. A trip can show you the door. Only practice walks you through it, and keeps you on the other side.

— What happens in the brain —
Science: When the mind wanders and circles around itself, a set of brain regions called the default mode network is active. Experienced meditators show less activity in this network, during meditation and at rest (Brewer et al., PNAS, 2011). Self-focus is a pattern of the brain, and patterns can be trained.
Science: Awe makes the self smaller. People who stood among giant trees felt smaller and acted more generously afterwards (Piff et al., Journal of Personality and Social Psychology, 2015). Across cultures, awe produces the same 'small self' (Bai et al., 2017).
Science: Talking to yourself in the third person, with your own name instead of 'I', lowers the emotional heat of a hard moment and makes wiser decisions easier (Kross & Ayduk, 2017).

— The six tools —
- Silence. Ten minutes a day without input: no music, no phone, no podcast. You watch thoughts come and go. The ego lives on noise.
- Breath. Slow breathing with a long exhale. When the body calms down, the urge to defend yourself drops with it.
- Cold. In cold water the ego has no opinion. There is only the breath and the next second. Only as far as your health allows.
- Awe. Once a week, go somewhere big: the sea, a mountain, a forest, the night sky. Stay until you feel small.
- Service. Do something for someone who cannot pay you back, and tell nobody.
- Your own name. When you are angry or hurt, talk about the situation in your head with your name instead of 'I': what is Paul going to do now?

— Three words —
The fastest daily ego kill is three words: I was wrong. No 'but'. No explanation. Say it once this week to someone it costs you something to say it to.
Watch what happens in your chest. That burning is the ego losing a little ground. The second time burns less. By the tenth time, you are the man in the room others trust, because he is the one who can admit it.

— What it is not —
- Not self-hatred. You do not fight the ego by attacking yourself. That is the ego again, in a darker suit.
- Not losing your confidence. A man without the ego in charge stands more firmly, because he no longer needs applause to stand.
- Not becoming passive. You still compete, build, lead and say no. You just stop doing it to prove something.
- Not a spiritual show. The man who tells everyone that he has no ego has found a new one.

═══ E2 — WHAT THE EGO IS ═══
— Definition —
The ego is the story you tell about yourself, and the part of you that defends that story. It says: I am the one who is right, the one who earns more, the one women want, the one who never fails.
The ego is not your identity. Your identity is what you do, day after day. The ego is what you need others to believe about you.

— How to recognise it —
- You need to win the argument more than you need the truth.
- Someone else's success feels like your loss.
- Criticism feels like an attack on who you are, not on what you did.
- In your stories you are always the hero or the victim.
- You compare: money, body, women, followers.
- You cannot ask for help, because asking would show that you need it.

— A strong self is not ego —
A man needs a strong self: confidence, boundaries, a clear no. That is not ego. The difference is the audience. A strong self does not need one. The ego cannot live without it. Confidence says: I can. The ego says: I am better than you.
Science: Psychologists describe a 'quiet ego': a self that is neither too loud nor too weak, that sees its own view and the view of others, and keeps growing (Wayment & Bauer, 2008). The goal of PURE is not to have no self. It is a quiet one.

— The ego's blind spot —
Science: The less skilled people are in an area, the more they tend to overestimate themselves in it (Kruger & Dunning, Journal of Personality and Social Psychology, 1999). The ego is loudest exactly where it knows least.
That is why you notice ego in others first. Theirs is loud to you. Yours is the water you swim in. The moment you are sure that someone else has a big ego, ask what in you it touches.

— The ego with women —
With women the ego shows in two forms: chasing to be confirmed, or going cold to stay safe. Both are about you, not about her.
A man without the ego in charge can stay present when she is upset, without needing to fix it, win it or run from it. That presence is rarer, and more attractive, than any line or any status.

— The daily check —
- Before you speak in a conflict: do I want to be right, or do I want this to go right?
- Before you post something: who is this really for?
- When you feel envy: what does this show me that I want for myself?

═══ EF — FUCK AROUND, FIND OUT, CREATE ═══
— The curve —
Next to the ego pillar, Paul drew a curve. At the bottom stand 80% of men: fuck around. They try things with money, sex, relationships and life, and never learn from the result.
The curve rises: 10%, 4%, 3%, 2 to 1%. At the top stand 0.1%: find out, and then create.

— Every experiment is a gain or a loss —
Every decision is an experiment. Money, sex, relationships, life: each one ends in a plus or a minus. Most men feel the minus and stop, or they repeat the same mistake with a new woman, a new investment, a new diet.
The few who rise go through the gains and the losses, again and again, until they find out what works. Then they stop experimenting blindly and start to create.

— Why most men stay at the bottom —
- They do not look at the result honestly, because the ego cannot bear having been wrong.
- They change the circumstances instead of themselves: new city, new woman, same pattern.
- They want the result without paying for the losses.
- They stop at the first minus.

— How to find out —
- Before you start, say out loud what you expect to happen.
- After, look at what really happened. No excuses, no story.
- Name one lesson. Only one.
- Next time, change one thing, not everything.
Science: Experts are not made by repetition alone but by deliberate practice: focused attempts at the edge of your ability, with feedback and correction (Ericsson et al., Psychological Review, 1993). Fuck around is repetition without feedback. Find out is practice with feedback.

— Create —
At the top of the curve a man stops reacting to life and starts building it: a body, a family, a business, a work that outlasts him. Creating is what comes when the lessons have been paid for.
Every minus is tuition. You do not get the money back. But you never have to pay it twice.

═══ E5 — SOMETHING GREATER THAN YOU ═══
— The roof —
Above the four pillars is the roof: something that is greater than you. God, the universe, nature, your family line, your people, humanity. What you call it does not matter, and nobody can tell you what to believe.
What matters is this: there is something you serve that is bigger than your own comfort.

— Why it is the end boss —
Everything before was about controlling yourself: body, mind, responsibility, ego. The end boss is the moment you stop revolving around yourself.
A man who only serves himself breaks when life hits him hard, because there is nothing left to stand up for. A man who serves something greater has a reason to get up again.

— What science sees —
Science: Over 14 years, people with a stronger sense of purpose in life were less likely to die, at every age, after accounting for other factors (Hill & Turiano, Psychological Science, 2014). The study shows a link, not a cause, but it is a strong one.
Science: People who spent money on others became happier than people who spent the same money on themselves (Dunn, Aknin & Norton, Science, 2008). Giving is not only good for the one who receives.

— How to find it —
- Ask yourself: who would miss me if I were gone, and what exactly would they miss?
- Notice what makes you forget time and forget yourself.
- Notice what makes you angry about the world. Your mission often hides there.
- Serve before you know. Help, give, protect. Meaning usually comes after the action, not before it.

— Family as the first roof —
For many men the first thing greater than themselves is a family: a woman, children, parents. Paul says it plainly: in the long run only one thing counts, and that is family and children.

— What it is not —
- Not a religion PURE gives you. You bring your own, or none.
- Not an excuse to neglect yourself. You cannot carry anything greater on a broken body.
- Not something you prove to others. It is quiet.
