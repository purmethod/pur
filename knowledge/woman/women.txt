PURE FOR A WOMAN
PURE Method · what differs for a woman · the method itself (four pillars, the roof, trust, the core rule) is the same for everyone

═══ CORE PRINCIPLE ═══
PURE was built by a man, for men, and it works for a woman too. The pillars stay. What changes is how each level meets her body and her life. Where the other knowledge files speak of "a man", read "a person", unless the point is about the male body.

═══ GIVING AND ENJOYING (PAUL) ═══
A woman does not only want to be given material gifts by her man. She wants to be given to physically.
The same holds for her as for him: she pampers her man not to get something back, not to receive anything. She has to be able to enjoy it herself.
Through the breathing, the cold and the right food she reaches places where she can enjoy physically without wanting anything.
(This is Paul's experience. Present it as his experience, not as a study.)

═══ P0 FOR A WOMAN ═══
Porn is not pleasure. Porn is pure anticipation. It drains the same system that drives her ambition, exactly as it does for a man (Paul).
Sexual control for her means where her sexual energy and her attention go, awareness of her own body, her pelvic floor, her desire, and what she allows in intimacy.
Pelvic floor training supports continence and sexual function in women.
Never give her male protocols: no ejaculation control, no morning erections, no testosterone targets.

═══ BODY ACROSS THE MONTH ═══
Her energy, sleep and appetite change across her cycle. She learns to notice her own pattern and plans around it. Training or eating by cycle phase is not proven; never present it as proven.
Strength training protects her muscles and her bones for life. She starts with the ladder like everyone.
Fasting stays gentle for her: long or hard fasting can disturb a woman's cycle. No fasting with an irregular cycle, in pregnancy, while breastfeeding, or with any history of an eating disorder.

═══ RELATIONSHIPS ═══
Relationship leadership for her means how she leads herself in a relationship: her boundaries, what she expects from a man, how she stays calm and clear.
Paul's stance, family and children matter most in the long run, is said once where it fits her goal, never as pressure.

═══ EGO ═══
For her, ego and comparison often run through looks, her body, social media and other women's lives.
