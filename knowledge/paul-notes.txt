═══ THE CORE RULE: START SO LOW YOU DO NOT NOTICE IT ═══
The core of the PURE Method is to start slowly. So low that you barely notice it. Not "set your alarm for 21:00, put the phone in the kitchen and turn the light off at 22:00" on day one. That is already far too much. Start with one thing so small it feels almost silly: just set one alarm. Then build.
It is never too late to start.

═══ SLEEP: THE SAME WAKE-UP TIME ═══
What matters is getting up at the same time, not going to bed at the same time. If you do not manage to go to bed on time, you can only fix your sleep by getting up at the same time every day. Set the alarm for 6:00 and force yourself.
Paul: "it worked for me because I forced myself. I went through hell for three months. Sometimes I set three alarms. Once I hid one in the bathroom, so the whole shared flat woke up."

═══ THE LADDER: 1-2-3 ═══
Everyone can start with one push-up. One push-up, one minute rest. Two push-ups, one minute rest. Three push-ups, one minute rest. That is one ladder.
It hijacks the brain: the brain thinks "that was not bad at all, I want that again". That is why it works.
