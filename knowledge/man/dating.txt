Not from the ranks of women who were already manipulated and traumatised by men before. The power to choose a woman who has not been broken, who has not been destroyed.

═══ HOW TO GET OPTIONS ═══
How a man gets those options is what Paul trains in his 1:1 coaching. In the book, mention this at most once, where it fits his goal with women, without a price or a link. Never turn it into a sales pitch.
The groundwork is in r3: date several women in parallel with honest intentions, choose by character, never manipulate, never pay for attention.

═══ HOW TO WRITE IT ═══
Write this in Paul's voice, as his conviction and experience, not as a study. Respect every woman. The point is a man's honesty and his choice, never contempt for women.
