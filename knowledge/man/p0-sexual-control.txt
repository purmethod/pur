═══ THE PELVIC FLOOR ═══
The pelvic floor is the most important muscle group in male sexual function. It controls erection quality, ejaculatory control, bladder function, and sexual stamina. Most men have never deliberately trained it. Most men don’t even know it exists as a trainable muscle.

The pelvic floor consists of the pubococcygeus (PC) muscle and surrounding muscles that form the base of the pelvis. In men, this muscle group directly controls:

- The rigidity of erections (blood retention in the corpus cavernosum)
- The timing and force of ejaculation
- The ability to separate orgasm from ejaculation
- Urinary control and prostate health

Science: Dorey et al., 2005 (BJU International; randomised trial, 55 men with erectile dysfunction). After three months of pelvic floor training with a physiotherapist, erections were clearly better than in the control group. After six months, 40% had regained normal function and a further 35% had improved. A small trial, but the direction is clear: the pelvic floor is trainable, and training it matters.

═══ THE KEGEL PROTOCOL ═══
Named after Dr. Arnold Kegel, these contractions are the training method for the pelvic floor.

PROTOCOL:

- 100 contractions every second day (muscle needs 24-48h recovery like any muscle)
- First thing in the morning, before feet touch the floor
- Nobody sees it. Nobody knows. You are training while the world sleeps.
- Full contraction — hold 2 seconds — full release
- The RELEASE is as important as the contraction. Most men only train the squeeze. The release trains control.

PROGRESSION:
Weeks 1-4: 100 slow contractions (2s hold, 2s release)
Weeks 5-8: 50 slow + 50 fast pulses
Weeks 9+: Add resistance — contract during cold shower, during breathing exercises

WHAT YOU WILL NOTICE:

- Stronger morning erections within 2-3 weeks
- Greater ejaculatory control within 4-6 weeks
- Increased sensitivity and body awareness
- The ability to approach the point of no return and pull back

═══ NON-EJACULATORY ORGASM ═══
Advanced practice. The body is capable of full orgasmic experience without ejaculation — this is documented in Taoist, Tantric, and ancient Hindu traditions. Modern neuroscience confirms the mechanism: orgasm and ejaculation are two separate neurological events that happen to occur together by default.

The pelvic floor is the switch. A trained pelvic floor can redirect the orgasmic energy upward through the spine rather than outward through ejaculation.

Traditions that teach this:

- Taoist sexuality (Mantak Chia — The Multi-Orgasmic Man)
- Tantric practice (Ananda Marga tradition)
- Islamic traditions of sexual restraint and conscious intimacy
- Buddhist practices of energy cultivation

What science says: orgasm and ejaculation are run by different parts of the nervous system and can happen separately; some men experience orgasm without ejaculation, for example after certain surgeries. Whether it can be trained on purpose has hardly been studied. Treat it as an experience to explore, never as a promise.

═══ TESTOSTERONE: WHAT REALLY MOVES IT ═══
Testosterone drives energy, drive, muscle and desire. The internet is full of tricks to raise it. Most of them are myths. These levers have evidence behind them:

Sleep. Science: Leproult & Van Cauter, 2011 (JAMA) — one week of five hours of sleep per night lowered daytime testosterone in healthy young men by 10 to 15%. A small study, but sleep is the cheapest testosterone there is.
Body fat. Science: Corona et al., 2013 (European Journal of Endocrinology, meta-analysis) — in men with obesity, losing weight raised testosterone, and the more weight lost, the larger the rise.
Training. Regular strength training with large muscle groups keeps muscle and hormone signalling healthy.
Alcohol and stress. Regular heavy drinking and chronic stress pull testosterone down.

Abstinence: the often cited Zhejiang study (2003) on testosterone peaking on day seven of abstinence was retracted in 2021. There is no solid evidence that holding back ejaculation raises testosterone over time. The reason to manage sexual energy is control, focus, presence and connection, not a hormone number.

Weak morning erections or a desire that has faded can be an early warning sign. Science: Thompson et al., 2005 (JAMA) — men who developed erectile dysfunction had a higher risk of later heart and vessel events. A man with these signs gets blood pressure, blood sugar, blood fats and testosterone checked by a doctor.

This is not celibacy. This is management.

═══ PORNOGRAPHY — THE DOPAMINE POISON ═══
Pornography is not a sexual outlet. It is a dopamine delivery system that rewires the brain away from the body.

When a man watches pornography:

- His brain releases massive dopamine surges in response to visual novelty
- His pelvic floor is disengaged — he is in his head, not his body
- His brain learns to associate sexual arousal with a screen, not with physical sensation
- Over time, real sexual experience feels less stimulating than the screen

Science: Voon et al., 2014 (PLOS ONE, Cambridge) — in men with compulsive sexual behaviour, sexual videos activated the same reward network (ventral striatum, dorsal anterior cingulate, amygdala) that responds to drug cues in addiction. They wanted it more, without liking it more. Wanting without liking is the signature of a habit that has taken over.

Many young men report that erections with a real partner become weaker while porn stays easy, and that it returns after they stop. The research on this is young and debated, but the pattern is common enough to take seriously.

The solution is not willpower. The solution is understanding. Once you understand that pornography is literally rewiring your brain against your own body, the decision to stop becomes obvious.
